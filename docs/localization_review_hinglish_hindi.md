# Hinglish and Hindi wording for review

Status: **DRAFT** for all Hinglish and Hindi rows. Written by the assistant, not yet read by a fluent speaker
(`REVIEW_STATUS` in `app/tools/ticket_texts.py` and `app/tools/lookup_texts.py` says so and a test pins it).
English is the reference and is exactly what the engine said before localisation.

These are the 25 fixed, code-owned sentences added in the final correction cycle. Placeholders in braces are filled by
code: order and item IDs, ticket IDs, counts and the customer's own description are never translated. The word `cancel`
(typed by the customer) and the IDs stay as written. Times elsewhere in the product stay in English UTC text.

Please check, for each row: (1) it reads naturally, (2) the tone is polite and the same level of formality across rows
(the draft uses "aap"/"कृपया" and "main" for the assistant), (3) it does not promise more than the English (a ticket is a
request for review and no outcome is promised), (4) the Hinglish spelling is what customers would write.

Known trade-offs: in Hinglish, common English words (ticket, order, confirm, support, agent, account) are kept because
customers write them that way; in Hindi, `टिकट`, `सपोर्ट` and `ऑर्डर` are transliterations. `lead_limit` drops the English
{limit} phrase in Hinglish and Hindi because it cannot be fitted into the sentence without an English fragment.
